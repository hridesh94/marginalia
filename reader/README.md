# Marginalia — ebook reader

A single-file, mobile-first ebook reader (`index.html`). It renders books with epub.js and keeps the library, reading position, highlights, bookmarks and settings in the browser (IndexedDB for book files, localStorage for everything else).

- **Any common ebook format.** Files are recognised by their content, not their extension. EPUB is kept as-is (embedded fonts are de-obfuscated so they display). MOBI/AZW/AZW3, PDF, FB2/FBZ, CBZ, TXT and HTML are converted to EPUB once, at import, so highlights, search, bookmarks and positions work the same everywhere. DRM-protected books (Kindle store, Adobe, Apple, LCP) and KFX are refused with a clear message.
- **The book's own design by default.** Publisher fonts, sizes, spacing, alignment and headings are left alone, with readability defaults where the book sets nothing: 1.5 line spacing, kerning and ligatures, hyphenation (the book's language is filled in when missing, so it works), balanced headings, and a column width that keeps lines near 65 characters. Books with cramped line spacing (under ~1.35) are opened up to 1.45. More options overrides font, line spacing and alignment one at a time ("Book" restores the original). Fixed-layout books (PDF, comics, fixed-layout EPUB) render as printed and centred. Right-to-left books turn the right way.
- **Page turns.** Flip (default), Slide, Fade or None. Swipes follow the finger and can be cancelled. Reduced-motion settings switch to Fade.
- **Offline dictionary.** Look Up uses WordNet 3.1 bundled with the Android app: base forms and irregular forms ("wept" finds "weep"), phrases, examples, tappable similar words, speak aloud, and a Words list of saved words with the sentence each came from. Without the bundled files (a plain web copy) it falls back to an online dictionary.
- **Several books at once.** Add books accepts many files in one pick and reports what was added, what was already there and what couldn't open.
- **Its own look:** warm paper and ink colours, a deep teal accent, serif headings, solid outlined panels. Reading features follow Kindle: tap zones, pages left in chapter, time-left estimate, four-colour highlights with notes, dictionary lookup, bookmark ribbon, resume to the exact position. Six themes: Day, Sepia, E-ink, Ink, Dusk, Night. E-ink imitates a dedicated e-reader: low-glare grey page, one crisp ink colour with slightly heavier strokes, underlined links, flat outlined panels and instant page turns.

The MOBI (foliate-js), PDF (pdf.js) and zlib (fflate) converters load on demand from jsDelivr; the Android build bundles them instead.

The file has no `<html>`/`<head>` wrapper of its own. The Android build (`android-app/scripts/build-web.mjs`) wraps it in a full document; to host it on the web, wrap it the same way, with a `viewport-fit=cover` viewport meta.
