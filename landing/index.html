Apple-inspired ebook reader for Android

Marginalia takes the calm of Apple Books and gives it a look of its own on Android. It opens EPUB, Kindle, PDF and comic files, shows each book the way its publisher designed it, and turns pages like paper. Your books stay on your phone.

Formats

Opens the books you already have

Marginalia reads each file's contents to work out what it is, so a book named .zip or with no extension still opens. Anything that isn't an EPUB is converted once, when you add it.

.epub

Opens exactly as published, including the publisher's embedded fonts.

.mobi .azw3

DRM-free Kindle books, with their images, links and contents.

.pdf

Pages look exactly as printed, and you can still search and highlight the text.

.cbz

Comics one page at a time. Manga reads right to left.

.fb2 .fbz

FictionBook files, including Cyrillic ones, with footnotes and poems intact.

.txt

Plain text, split into chapters wherever a heading like “Chapter I” appears.

.html

Saved web pages and stories, with their own styles and images.

Open with

Tap a book in your Files app or downloads, then choose Marginalia.

Books with DRM can't be opened. This includes books bought from the Kindle store and Kindle KFX files. DRM-free Kindle files, like ones from free libraries, work fine.

Features

Designed like Apple Books, built for Android

The page shows only the text until you need more. Tap the middle of the screen to bring back the menu, the contents and your progress.

Page turns

Pages turn like paper

Tap the edge of the screen, or drag the page and it follows your finger. Let go early and it settles back into place. You can also choose Slide, Fade or no animation.

Typography

Every book looks the way it was designed

Marginalia keeps the publisher's fonts, spacing, alignment and headings. You can still change the typeface, line spacing or alignment, one setting at a time. Choose Book to go back to the original. Text is set ragged-right with even word spacing and no words split across lines, comfortable line lengths are kept, and books with cramped lines are opened up a little.

Themes

Six themes, including E-ink

Day, Sepia, Ink for bold, high-contrast type, Dusk and Night for reading in the dark. E-ink reads like a dedicated e-reader: a low-glare grey page that is neither bright nor dark, crisp heavier text and instant page turns. A built-in dimmer darkens the page without changing your screen brightness.

Highlights

Highlights, notes and bookmarks

Press and hold any text and a small bar appears: tap one of four colours to highlight it, or add a note, copy it or look it up. Colour-code passages as you read. Bookmarked pages show a ribbon, and everything you save is listed by chapter.

Dictionary

A dictionary built in

Look up any word without leaving the page, even with no internet. It finds the base form, so wept shows weep, gives an example for each meaning and lists similar words you can tap. It can also say the word aloud and save it with the sentence you found it in.

Progress

Always know where you are

See how many pages are left in the chapter, how far you are through the book, and how long it should take to finish at your reading speed. Every book reopens exactly where you stopped.

Android

Made for Android

Read full screen with the system bars out of the way, turn pages with the volume keys, keep the screen on while you read, search the whole book and share a quote to any app.

Privacy

Your books stay on your phone

There's no account and nothing is uploaded. Even the dictionary works offline.

Install

Install in about a minute

Marginalia isn't on the Play Store. The download button always gives you the newest version, straight from GitHub. No account needed.

  1. Download

    Tap Download for Android on your phone. Marginalia.apk saves to your Downloads folder.

  2. Open it

    Tap the finished download, or find Marginalia.apk in your Files app under Downloads.

  3. Allow the install

    Android asks once whether your browser or Files app may install apps. Allow it, then tap Install.

  4. Add your books

    Open Marginalia and tap Add books. You can pick several files at once. A short reading guide is already in your library.
